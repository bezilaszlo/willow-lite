## Why (decisions already made, do not re-litigate)

- The community kernel cannot be patched (binary only) and its recipe does not reproduce it (recipe issue #1: self-built kernel hangs, unpublished delta).
- Mainline has the willow DT from **v7.1** (absent in v7.0); v7.2 is the latest stable. The mainline NT36672A panel driver exists (Tianma, `tianma,fhd-video`) and has `unprepare`; mainline has no NT36xxx SPI touch driver and no `ktd3136` backlight driver.
- Earlier root-cause hypothesis for the Hyprland black panel: the community kernel initializes the panel out of band ("ginkgo UEFI DSI", "panel init complete", DSI retry loop) and tells DRM it is already prepared (`Skipping prepare of already prepared panel`), so a userspace modeset may not re-run init. Correction: the current mainline panel patch already sets `prepare_prev_first = true`; the older tracker claim that this flag was absent is stale. The latest EBBG trial stayed physically black with the touch module absent, so touch loading is not required for this failure. **Panel reset/init timing, power rails, and actual backlight emission remain unresolved; all but the user's black-screen observation are unverified.**
- Patches live in this repo (omarchy-mobile shape): `kernel/base.txt` + `kernel/patches/*.patch` + `kernel/config/willow.fragment`. No separate kernel fork. Do not touch `moarchy` or its PKGBUILDs; moarchy's "no kernel patches" policy is a separate open decision for the user.
- Device naming: ginkgo = Redmi Note 8, willow = Note 8T (adds NFC). This unit is the shenchao panel module. `docs/hardware.md` still says "Redmi Note 8, codename willow": reconcile later.

## Recovery facts (measured 2026-09-29)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Panic with `panic=30` (`echo c > /proc/sysrq-trigger` as root; the sysrq mask is 16 but the procfs trigger bypasses it) | Phone reached **fastboot by itself at t+38 s**. `boot-trial.sh` then brought it back; SSH up in 35 s. |
| True hang (`kernel.panic=0` then the same crash) | **No recovery in 180 s**; panel stayed lit and frozen. Needed a manual Power hold (10-15 s), then Volume Down + Power for fastboot. |

Consequences: panics recover, hangs do not. The community config has `QCOM_WDT`, `SOFTLOCKUP_DETECTOR`, `DETECT_HUNG_TASK` off and `PANIC_TIMEOUT=0` (the trial cmdline supplies `panic=30`). A frozen lit screen fools a lit/dark camera check, so detect hangs with SSH heartbeat or two camera frames a few seconds apart. Untested: what a hardware watchdog reset lands in (fastboot or stock boot), and whether Qualcomm's secure watchdog resets real bus hangs.

Manual recovery from a hang: hold Power 10-15 s, then hold Volume Down + Power to reach fastboot, then `scripts/boot-trial.sh`.

- **Touch WDT marker diagnosis (2026-09-29, source comparison):** diagnostic raw prefix is 18 bytes of `0xfd` repeated; no coordinates are reported as valid. Huabin/Novatek's `nvt_wdt_fw_recovery()` checks `point_data[1..6]` for consecutive all-`0xfd` or all-`0xfe` samples, triggering after >10; its threaded event handler then reads firmware history and force-reloads firmware, with `0xfe` additionally issuing the `SWRST_N8_ADDR=0xAA` MCU-idle reset. Current driver has no watchdog marker detector/recovery counter; the parser simply rejects the resulting IDs/status. Strongly indicates the captured stream is the vendor-documented WDT/recovery sentinel rather than an ordinary touch packet, while the cause of watchdog reset remains unknown. This is a high-confidence diagnosis but not yet a safe production fix: recovery requires reset/reload semantics, and the unsafe global `0x69` bootloader reset must remain excluded; the effects of `0xAA` and a force firmware reload on this Shenchao TDDI/panel path are unverified.
