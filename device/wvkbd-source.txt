The Sxmo maintainers maintain the upstream repository and use wvkbd for mobile Linux.

Willow patch: `device/patches/wvkbd-bottom-margin.patch` adds a bounded `--bottom-margin N` option (default 0) and applies a bottom margin to the layer surface. On the measured Hyprland 0.56 session, the compositor accounts for this margin in addition to an exclusive zone equal to keyboard height; adding the margin to the zone double-counted it and left an extra 30px app gap. The patched `main.c` SHA-256 is `787e0a64a64af388a4cd53df306800d307d20104b535efe1d9d3fb73415a27eb`. `scripts/build-wvkbd.sh` pins this upstream commit, verifies pristine and patched source hashes, builds on the phone, and stages only into a fresh package target under `/var/lib/willow-lite-wvkbd-bottom-margin-*`. It does not replace the live keyboard; deployment is separate.

2026-10-01 build dependencies were missing on the phone. `pkgconf`, `gcc`, `make`, and four resolved dependencies (`gc`, `guile`, `libisl`, `libmpc`) were installed from signed packages from the official Arch Linux ARM repository after the phone could not resolve its configured mirror. No system upgrade or repository change was made. The package and build staging artifacts were retained. A first source revision exited after SIGUSR2 because an unbraced guard surrounded the multi-statement `die()` macro; the later source fix uses an exclusive zone equal to height, as measured on this compositor. The deployed binary SHA-256 is `d4a23de8109b84b8980b2094013798d32646ae297460222971f42d73a067250e`. With margin 30, observed live geometry is keyboard y=830–1140, bottom gesture strip y=1140–1170, and tiled foot clients ending y=830. The physical bottom swipe and spacebar access have not yet been retested.
